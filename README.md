## Dashboard Features
# 📊 RETAIL SALES DASHBOARD

### 📈 Data Analysis & Visualization Project

Welcome to my **Retail Sales Dashboard Project!**

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data and presenting meaningful business insights through an interactive Excel dashboard.

The dashboard helps understand sales performance, customer behavior, product categories and monthly sales trends.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall retail sales performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Identify top-performing customers.
- Compare sales across different product categories.
- Track monthly sales trends.
- Analyze regional sales performance.

---

## 📂 Dataset Overview

The project uses customer transaction data containing:

- Transaction ID
- Date
- Customer ID
- Customer Name
- Product ID
- Product Name
- Category
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Payment Method
- Region
- Customer Segment
- Total Amount

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. 📊 Bar Chart – Sales by Category
Analyzes total sales generated by different product categories.

### 2. 🥧 Pie Chart – High vs Low Sales
Shows the distribution of high-sales and low-sales transactions.

### 3. 📈 Line Chart – Monthly Sales Trend
Displays monthly sales performance and helps identify sales trends.

### 4. 🏆 Top 10 Customers
Identifies the customers contributing the highest sales.

### 5. 👥 Customer Performance Table
Displays customer-wise sales, orders and quantity sold.

### 6. 🌍 Regional Sales Analysis
Compares sales performance across different regions.
